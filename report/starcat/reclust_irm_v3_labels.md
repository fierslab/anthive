## Cluster 0 (n = 4317)

**Signature gene highlights:** CX3CR1, TREM2, HTRA1, P2RY13, APOE, GPR34

**Interpreted biology:** This is the homeostatic-IRM backbone. CX3CR1 and P2RY13 are canonical homeostatic purinergic and fractalkine-axis receptors maintained in resting surveillance microglia (Butovsky 2014, Nature Neuroscience). TREM2 at +1.78 log2FC (pct_in = 0.94) marks TREM2-dependent phagocytic capacity without DAM-level upregulation; HTRA1 and SORL1 tag this population as having intact lysosomal and APP-processing function. APOE is expressed broadly but enriched here, consistent with homeostatic lipid-handling rather than disease-associated upregulation. The down-markers are striking: CCL8, CXCL10, CCL2 are all strongly absent, confirming this cluster is NOT actively secreting pro-inflammatory cytokines. This is the dominant IRM state — baseline interferon-primed surveillance without overt cytokine activation.

**Proposed label:** `IRM`

**Confidence:** high

**Caveats:**
- C10orf54 (also known as VSIR / VISTA) is the top marker by log2FC (+3.98, pct_in = 0.87). VISTA is an immune checkpoint receptor; its enrichment here suggests active suppression of inflammatory output, which is biologically coherent with the homeostatic interpretation but worth noting as a potentially interesting functional feature.
- FAM105A (OTULIN-related; linear deubiquitinase regulator) is the second marker — function in microglia is not well characterized. Do not over-interpret.
- The ATPIF1 signal (+2.46) could reflect mitochondrial activity but could also be a partial overlap with the dying-cell artifact in cluster 2. Check MT% for this cluster.

## Cluster 1 (n = 1198)

**Signature gene highlights:** CXCL10, CCL2, CCL8, IL1B, SOD2, NFKB2, S100A8/A9

**Interpreted biology:** This is an overtly activated, cytokine-producing IRM state. CXCL10 (+5.1 log2FC, 76% of cells) is the canonical type-I IFN response chemokine (interferon-gamma-inducible protein 10); CCL2 and CCL8 are monocyte-recruiting chemokines whose expression at this level indicates active immune engagement, not baseline IFN priming. IL1B (+4.5 log2FC, 95% cells) is a master pro-inflammatory cytokine requiring inflammasome processing for secretion. SOD2 (mitochondrial superoxide dismutase, +3.8) indicates oxidative stress management under cytokine load. NF-kB2 upregulation confirms canonical NF-kB pathway activation. S100A8/A9 (calprotectin complex, +3.6/+4.0) are innate immunity alarmins classically associated with myeloid activation. EBI3 is the IL-27B chain, and BCL2A1 is an anti-apoptotic gene induced by cytokine signaling. The down-markers — HTRA1, GPR34, TREM2, OLFML3, ZFP36L2 — are exactly the homeostatic genes depleted in cluster 0, confirming this as a distinctly activated counterpart. This cluster represents the bona fide interferon-plus-inflammatory-cytokine arm of IRM.

**Proposed label:** `IRM-inflammatory`

**Rationale for not using plain `IRM`:** The scale of CXCL10/CCL2/IL1B induction (log2FC > 4, >75% cells) puts this far beyond baseline IFN priming. Using plain IRM would conflate two mechanistically different populations. Sala-Frigerio 2019 (Cell Reports) and Hammond 2019 (Immunity) both distinguish IRM as ISG-high but cytokine-low; this cluster has both ISG and cytokine arms active simultaneously.

**Confidence:** high

**Caveats:**
- GPR84 (fatty acid receptor, bacterial LPS-responsive) is at +3.5 log2FC. This could indicate LPS-like stimulation from the xenograft microenvironment rather than purely IFN-driven activation. Worth checking what stimulus is driving this cluster.
- SLAMF7 (+4.3, 36% cells) is normally an NK/plasma cell marker; occasional myeloid expression is documented but warrants scrutiny — could indicate a sub-population with NK-like properties or low-level doublets with NK cells.
- This cluster uses scRNA-seq, so IL1B and CCL2 are genuinely captured. In snRNA-seq data this cluster would likely be invisible (see artifacts note).

## Cluster 3 (n = 286)

**!! UNCERTAIN IDENTITY — PROBABLE DOUBLETS OR MISASSIGNED CELLS !!**

**Signature gene highlights:** LINGO1 (+30.4), OOEP (+16.3), CXCL8 (+10.9), MECOM (+30.2), BDNF-AS (+8.9), AMBRA1 (+7.6)

**Interpreted biology:** This cluster's signature does not fit any known microglia state, and several top markers are biologically implausible for microglia. LINGO1 (leucine-rich repeat and Ig domain-containing, Nogo receptor-interacting protein 1) is a canonical oligodendrocyte and neuronal marker involved in myelination and axon regeneration — not expressed in normal microglia. OOEP (oocyte-expressed protein homolog) is testis/oocyte-enriched; its appearance in a brain microglia dataset is immediately suspicious. MECOM (MDS1 and EVI1 complex locus) is a hematopoietic transcription factor associated with myeloid leukemia and iPSC reprogramming — its very high enrichment (log2FC +30, pct_out = 0.00) in a small cluster of iPSC-derived cells is a red flag for reprogramming-related contamination or retention of an undifferentiated iPSC sub-population. CXCL8 (IL-8) is a neutrophil-recruiting chemokine rarely expressed in homeostatic microglia. AMBRA1 is an autophagy regulator with broad expression. BDNF-AS is a brain-enriched lncRNA antisense to BDNF. BAIAP2L1 is actin-remodeling and neuron-associated.

The most parsimonious explanations, in order of likelihood:
1. **Undifferentiated iPSC contaminants** — MECOM/OOEP/LINGO1 co-expression suggests cells that did not fully differentiate into microglia. This is a known issue in iPSC-microglia protocols.
2. **Doublets with neurons or oligodendrocytes** — LINGO1 + BDNF-AS + BAIAP2L1 together could represent microglia-neuron or microglia-oligodendrocyte doublets not removed by Scrublet/DoubletFinder.
3. A genuine stress or transitional state cannot be fully excluded but would require mechanistic evidence.

**Proposed label:** `unclear` (likely iPSC-contaminant or doublet)

**Confidence:** low

**Caveats:**
- Run DoubletFinder or Scrublet scores on this cluster specifically.
- Check expression of pluripotency markers (POU5F1, NANOG, SOX2) in these cells.
- Check whether MECOM is expressed in the original (pre-reclustered) parent dataset — if it is confined to this cluster only in the full dataset, the iPSC-contamination hypothesis is strongly supported.
- This cluster must not be labeled as any microglia state until its identity is resolved.

## Cluster 4 (n = 263)

**Signature gene highlights:** CD163, LILRB2, LILRB5, MARCO, IL1RN, DRAM1, ACSL1, TGFBI

**Interpreted biology:** This cluster has a coherent anti-inflammatory / tolerogenic macrophage signature that maps most closely to what the field calls CRM (context-responsive microglia) or alternatively-activated macrophages. CD163 (hemoglobin-haptoglobin scavenger receptor) is the canonical marker of M2-polarized/anti-inflammatory macrophages — it is barely expressed in homeostatic microglia and its expression at log2FC +6.1 in 97% of these cells is a strong functional signal. LILRB2 and LILRB5 are inhibitory leukocyte immunoglobulin-like receptors that suppress innate immune activation via ITIM-mediated signaling; their co-expression with LILRA5 (activating LILR) indicates these cells are fine-tuning immune thresholds. MARCO (macrophage receptor with collagenous structure) is a scavenger receptor associated with tolerogenic macrophage programs and apoptotic cell clearance. IL1RN (IL-1 receptor antagonist) is a direct anti-inflammatory effector that suppresses IL-1 signaling. DRAM1 is a damage-regulated autophagy modulator; ACSL1 (long-chain acyl-CoA synthetase 1) and BNIP3 (hypoxia-inducible mitophagy marker) suggest altered lipid metabolism and mitophagy. TGFBI and METRNL (meteorin-like, a tissue-remodeling cytokine) add a remodeling/resolving dimension. The down-markers are telling: CX3CR1, P2RY12, P2RY13, HTRA1, OLFML3 — the entire homeostatic surveillance program is suppressed.

**Proposed label:** `CRM`

**Important caveat on specificity:** In a xenograft model, CD163-high/CX3CR1-low cells could alternatively represent border-associated macrophages (BAMs) from the host mouse brain that were inadvertently captured and passed human-gene thresholds, or iPSC-microglia that have acquired a BAM-like phenotype in the xenograft niche. Mancuso 2023 should report human-cell selection strategy; this should be verified. Sun et al. 2023 (Cell) identified a CD163-enriched "lipid-processing" microglia cluster in AD; overlap with that signature should be checked.

**Confidence:** medium

**Caveats:**
- Verify these are human cells (human vs mouse transcript ratio). If BAM contamination occurred, this cluster would be human-gene-sparse and mouse-gene-rich.
- FCGR3A (CD16) is in the down-markers at −5.25 log2FC, distinguishing this from classical monocyte-derived macrophages which express CD16 strongly. That is biologically consistent with a tissue-resident lineage.
- RNASE1 (+4.9, 95% cells) is expressed in liver macrophages (Kupffer cells) and some perivascular macrophages — another marker that does not fit canonical parenchymal microglia identity and supports a BAM or alternative macrophage lineage interpretation.
- F13A1 (Factor XIIIa, coagulation transglutaminase) is highly expressed in dural/perivascular macrophages in multiple datasets. Its presence here strengthens the BAM hypothesis.

## Overall assessment

The five-cluster reclustering of IRM cells reveals a mixture of genuine biology and technical artifacts, which must be resolved before any scientific conclusions are drawn.

**Genuine biology (clusters 0 and 1):** The reclustering does recover two biologically meaningful sub-populations within IRM. Cluster 0 (63% of all cells) is the homeostatic IRM baseline — interferon-primed, phagocytically competent, cytokine-quiet. Cluster 1 (17%) is an overtly activated state with simultaneous ISG + NF-kB + cytokine output. These two clusters are not a continuum in a trivial sense: the down-regulation of TREM2, HTRA1, GPR34, and OLFML3 in cluster 1 (the entire Sala-Frigerio homeostatic module) while IL1B and CCL2 surge indicates a genuine transcriptional state transition, not gradient noise. This distinction has functional consequences for interpretation of the parent Mancuso 2023 IRM annotation.

**Technical artifacts (cluster 2):** This cluster is dead/low-quality cells and must be excluded. Its presence means the upstream QC was insufficient or the MT% threshold was too permissive. The 856 cells in this cluster should not be counted in any downstream analysis.

**Uncertain (cluster 3):** 286 cells with non-microglial markers including LINGO1 and OOEP. Most likely iPSC differentiation contaminants or doublets. This must be resolved with doublet detection and pluripotency marker checks — it cannot be labeled.

**Out-of-vocabulary (cluster 4):** The CD163/LILRB/MARCO/IL1RN cluster does not fit IRM, HLA, DAM, or HM. CRM is the closest label in the user's vocabulary, but the profile is unusual enough (F13A1, RNASE1, CD163 at very high levels, CX3CR1 fully suppressed) that BAM contamination or a xenograft-specific macrophage state should be actively excluded before committing to any label.

**No label collision** between clusters 0 and 1 — they are clearly distinct. **No DAM or HLA signal** was detected in any cluster: no SPP1, no MHC-II upregulation, no GPNMB. If DAM or HLA sub-populations exist within this dataset's IRM cells, they are not resolved at this clustering resolution or they are genuinely absent from this xenograft preparation.

**Recommendation:** Exclude cluster 2 from all downstream analyses. Resolve cluster 3 identity with doublet detection. Validate cluster 4 BAM vs CRM distinction with human/mouse barcode separation. Proceed to label clusters 0 and 1 as IRM and IRM-inflammatory respectively — these two are the scientifically defensible findings from this reclustering.
