Connect an AI client (MCP)

MCP lets an AI assistant such as Claude, Cursor or VS Code search and plot the anthive datasets on your behalf. You get one private link that identifies you. Paste it into your client once and it keeps working until you rotate or revoke it.

Treat this link like a password. Anyone who has it can query the data as you. If you think it leaked, rotate it: the old link stops working immediately.
