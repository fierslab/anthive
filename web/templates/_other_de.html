These are differential expression results that were stored with the dataset: the authors' own analysis as published, or an analysis added by the lab. Each study has its own method (cell-level models, pseudobulk tests), its own covariates and its own definition of the groups, so its numbers are not on the same footing as the donor-level facet tests above and are never counted towards replication.

Each row is one term and contrast of a study. A cell counts the {{ noun }} with an adjusted p below 0.05, split into higher and lower by the sign of the effect. Cell-level models give very small p values with many cells, so large counts are expected there. Intercept rows are left out. Click a cell for its {{ noun }}; the list header links the full volcano and MA plots of that contrast.
