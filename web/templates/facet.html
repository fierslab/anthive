Host animals, not donors. The replicate is the mouse. This test detects large effects only (a handful of animals per genotype), and the cells were isolated live, so dissociation-response genes are suspect. Glossary{{ doc_toggle('fx-app-nlgf-about', 'About the App NL-G-F comparison') }}

{% call doc_block('About the App NL-G-F comparison', id='fx-app-nlgf-about') %}

The facet contrasts App NL-G-F knock-in host mice with control hosts in xenograft studies of human microglia. The control is stated on every unit (the humanised-App host, App hu/hu, isolates the three familial AD mutations; a wild-type host does not). Animals are compared within one grafted human line and one timepoint, because the amyloid response grows with age and differs between lines; a stratum where genotype cannot be separated from line or timepoint is not tested. The genotype of the grafted cells themselves is the facet ant_genotype; the host's is ant_host_genotype.

Few animals mean wide intervals and low power: a gene that is not detected here is not shown to be unchanged. Strata of one dataset (lines, timepoints) share one vote in the replication count, so a study counts once.
