What the flags mean
replicate level (donor)
Tested on pseudobulks per replicate (donor, animal or sample). The replicate, not the cell, is the unit.
cell-level, descriptive only
Too few replicates for a test (fewer than 3 in a level, or no replicate column). Cells are not replicates, so the numbers are descriptive and carry no p or q.
descriptive only
A level has fewer than 5 replicates (Braak: fewer than 10). Estimates without p, q or confidence interval.
provisional
The disease vocabulary is not settled; what counts as control differs between studies.
composition
The fraction of each donor's cells in this cell type varies with the facet (p < 0.05, uncorrected). The effect may reflect which cells were captured.
batch confounded
Facet and batch cannot be separated; batch was left out of the model.
pooled
Several files of one cohort pooled per donor into one unit.
donor overlap not checked
Whether this cohort shares donors with another cohort was never checked. It is counted, and agreement with the other cohorts may be partly shared donors.
reanalysis, not counted
A reanalysis of cells another dataset serves: shown, never counted.

Methods.
  • Unit of analysis: the donor. Raw counts are summed per donor within one cell type stratum (at least 10 cells per donor); a stratum is the dataset's cell type annotation, or all cells when the dataset holds one cell type.
  • Genes: edgeR filterByExpr, TMM, limma-voom, lmFit and eBayes; model expression ~ facet + sex + age_z + pmi_z [+ batch], covariates only when known for at least 90% of at least 15 donors. Benjamini-Hochberg within one dataset, stratum and facet. A second test, limma treat, asks whether the change exceeds tau = log2(1.1) (a 10% change; Braak per stage log2(1.1)/3); its q is shown as q (≥10% change).
  • Modules: per donor score (mean of the per-cell score, or a score on the pseudobulk), OLS per module, permutations of the facet labels across donors (10,000 by default; the model line of each row gives the number used); q_param and q_perm by Benjamini-Hochberg within one dataset, stratum, facet, method and score source.
  • Contrasts: sex m vs f, disease AD vs control (provisional), APOE e4 carrier vs non-carrier, Braak as a linear trend per stage. A level with fewer than 5 donors (Braak: fewer than 10 donors) is descriptive only.
  • Replication across cohorts: one anchor unit per cohort and cell type group; the one-sided treat p values of the anchors are combined per gene by a Benjamini and Heller partial conjunction (Simes, at least 2 cohorts) and adjusted by Benjamini-Hochberg over the genes of the facet and cell type group (the r-value, replicated when below 0.05). The count k of cohorts with q (≥10% change) below 0.05 is descriptive. Only pathology-based disease definitions are combined.
  • Each row states its own model, covariates and run under "details". Rows are separate tests; units of one cohort are not summed.
