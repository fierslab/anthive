Methods.
  • Unit of analysis: the donor. Raw counts are summed per donor within one cell type stratum (at least 10 cells per donor); a stratum is the dataset's cell type annotation, or all cells when the dataset holds one cell type.
  • Genes: edgeR filterByExpr, TMM, limma-voom, lmFit and eBayes; model expression ~ facet + sex + age_z + pmi_z [+ batch], covariates only when known for at least 90% of at least 15 donors. Benjamini-Hochberg within one dataset, stratum and facet.
  • Modules: per donor score (mean of the per-cell score, or a score on the pseudobulk), OLS per module, 1,000 permutations of the facet labels across donors; q_param and q_perm by Benjamini-Hochberg within one dataset, stratum, facet and method.
  • Contrasts: sex m vs f, disease AD vs control (provisional), APOE e4 carrier vs non-carrier, Braak as a linear trend per stage. A level with fewer than 5 donors (Braak: fewer than 10 donors) is descriptive only.
  • Each row states its own model, covariates and run under "details". No pooling across datasets: a cross-dataset meta-analysis comes later, and donors overlap between datasets.
