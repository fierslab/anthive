{% call doc_block('How the search works', id='search-how') %}

One search covers datasets, collections, genes, gene sets, papers, artefacts{% if ui.facets %}, facets{% endif %} and the help pages, and only what you may see. Filler words ("expression", "in", "across", "which", "datasets") are dropped. A word that is the name of a gene is shown as a gene and does not filter the datasets, because nearly every dataset measures nearly every gene; open the gene to see its expression in every dataset. A word that names a disease, cell type, organism or tissue recorded in the catalogue must match that field, so "microglia" finds datasets recorded as microglia and not a neuron dataset whose description mentions microglia. Every other word must occur in a dataset's title, collection, description, method or the names of its cell annotation columns. A collection is listed when its own text holds every word or when it holds a matching dataset. Artefacts match on their title, description, kind and what they belong to; facets on their name and definition; help on the text of each topic and glossary term. A word also matches inside a longer word ("microglia" finds "microglial cell"), a plural s is optional, and AD, PD, MS, ALS, FTD and APOE4 are expanded. A dataset that has a column such as "APOE e4 carrier" is listed even if few of its donors carry the allele; open it to see the values.
