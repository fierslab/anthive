{# The one effect bar of the whole site (window.antEffectBar), loaded by base.html. A horizontal diverging bar from a zero line on a FIXED scale per unit, so bars of different rows, pages and facets compare by eye. Used by the forests of the gene view, the gene set view and the dataset Facets tab and by the summary tables of the facet overview. No page draws its own effect marks. Scales (full half-width of the track): log2 fold change two-group +/-1 (faint tick at 10 percent = 0.14), per Braak stage +/-0.3, per decade +/-0.3, per e4 allele +/-0.5 SD of the donor score two-group +/-1, per Braak stage +/-0.3, per decade +/-0.3, per e4 allele +/-0.5 Values beyond the scale are capped: the bar runs to the edge and ends in an arrowhead. Colour is the sign (up warm, down cool, the same two hues everywhere), grey when the row is not counted or only descriptive. Filled means the cohort's own q is below 0.05, open means it is not. The thin whisker is the 95 percent CI in a forest and the min to max over the counted cohorts in a summary table. API: antEffectBar.scaleKey(facet, measure) measure 'lfc' or 'sd'; any `scale` argument may also be an object {max, unit} for a list scaled to its own values (the dataset Facet > Gene and GS lists) antEffectBar.barHtml({scale, est, lo, hi, sig, counted, descriptive, whisker: 'ci' | 'range', title}) antEffectBar.axisHtml(scale) header cell content: unit, then -max, 0, +max once antEffectBar.numberHtml(est, scale, q) signed number with q beside it antEffectBar.legendHtml(scale, kind) one footnote line, kind 'forest' or 'summary' #} {% macro effect_bar_assets() -%} {%- endmacro %}
