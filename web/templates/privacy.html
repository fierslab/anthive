Chat assistant — privacy & data handling

Draft wording — under review. The mechanics are live; the text below may change.

This note covers the in-app data assistant (the chat box). It does not change how the rest of the catalogue works.

What we store

  • Every message you send, the assistant's replies, the tool calls it runs and their results, and per-message token/cost counts.
  • Held in a database on this server, linked to your KU Leuven (Authelia) login, kept indefinitely.

Who can see it

The deployment admins (Mark Fiers & co.), for debugging, improving the assistant, and reviewing feedback you flag. Not public; not shared with third parties beyond the AI provider below.

Who processes your prompts

To generate a reply, your message plus catalogue context are sent to OpenRouter, which routes to an upstream model provider (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, … depending on the chosen model). These providers have their own retention and training policies — assume your text may be retained unless an opt-out is configured.

What we never send

Your single-cell data itself never leaves this server. The tools return summarised numbers and chart links to the model — never raw expression matrices or cell-level data.

Your controls

  • If you don't open the chat, or don't send a message, nothing is transmitted.
  • To review or delete your stored chat history, contact Mark Fiers.

Legal basis

To confirm — internal research tool, processed under GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) (legitimate interest). This line will be finalised by the data owner.
