{% call doc_block('About open reports', id='jo-help') %}

Anyone who signs in can report a problem in the journal of a dataset, collection, gene set, facet or artefact. This page lists the reports across the site that are not closed yet, so curators can work through them and users can see what is already known before relying on a dataset.

A data error is a mistake in the data or its metadata, an annotation concern a doubtful cell type label, a data request something people would like added. Admins, curators and a dataset's owner acknowledge, fix or decline them on the dataset's Journal tab; a fixed data error or annotation concern names the changelog line and the new dataset version. You only see reports on things you can see. More in the help.
